How it works

Your query is converted into a vector embedding and compared against all indexed document chunks using cosine similarity. The closest matching chunks are returned ranked by relevance score.

  • Use natural language — questions, phrases, or keywords all work.
  • A higher score (closer to 1.0) means a stronger semantic match.
  • Use the Top N selector to control how many results are returned.
  • Rate results with 👍 / 👎 to help track retrieval quality over time.
